Jakiur Rahman's engineering career began in 2018 at Genuine Software Solutions, where he joined as a junior PHP developer. Working on real production systems from day one instilled in him a rigorous approach to code quality, database design, and server reliability that still defines his work today.

In 2020, he moved to Techpirates, where he specialised in building commercial WordPress plugins — including licensing systems, WooCommerce extensions, and SaaS-connected integrations sold to thousands of businesses. This period sharpened his ability to write plugin architecture that is both extensible and maintainable under real commercial pressure.

In 2023, he joined Kodezen as a PHP Developer, quickly expanding his scope to include DevOps responsibilities: containerisation with Docker, CI/CD automation, VPS provisioning, and infrastructure monitoring. Within a year he was promoted to Senior PHP Developer (2024), and by 2025 to Team Lead — guiding a cross-functional engineering team delivering complex SaaS and CRM products.

In 2026, he founded Lunova Digital, a software engineering agency that brings together Laravel development, DevOps, Android, automation, and SaaS expertise under one roof, serving clients across Europe, North America, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East.
